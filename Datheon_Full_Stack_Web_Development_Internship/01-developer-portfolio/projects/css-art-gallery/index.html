Six drawings, zero images

Every picture here is built from empty divs, borders, box-shadows and pseudo-elements. Hover or tap a drawing to animate it.

Tabby cat

Border triangles for ears and nose, box-shadow for the second eye and whiskers. Hover to make it blink.

Night owl

Eye rings come from stacked box-shadows. Hover and the pupils follow you.

Sunrise ridge

Mountains are border triangles, the snow cap is a pseudo-element. Hover to lift the sun.

Moonlit hills

The crescent is one inset box-shadow, and six stars share a single element. Hover to twinkle.

Eclipse rings

One 40px circle with four spread shadows. Hover to push the rings off-centre.

Nine squares

A single square plus eight shadow offsets. Hover to turn the grid into dots.
