How to get the full 40 hours

  1. Let the first burn run 3 hours.The wax should melt all the way to the edge so the candle does not tunnel.
  2. Trim the wick to 5 mm.Do it before every burn to keep the flame steady and the glass clean.
  3. Keep burns under 4 hours.Let the candle cool and set before you light it again.
  4. Reuse the jar.Soak it in hot water, wipe it out and use it for pens or plants.
